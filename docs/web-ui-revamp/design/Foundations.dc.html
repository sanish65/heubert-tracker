Controls

Shape

Cards 16 · inner tiles 10–12 · inputs & buttons 10 · dialogs 20 · pills and avatars round. 4px spacing grid; card padding 20–24; page gutter 32.

Motion

Cards lift 2px with a deeper shadow in 180ms. The aurora drifts slowly; festival scenes and the champion badge play only with Animations on, and never under reduced motion.

Icons & emoji

Line icons, 1.8px stroke, rounded joins (Lucide-style). Emoji leave the chrome — nav, headings and buttons; they stay only where people type them, like retro cards.

Layout

Fluid at every width: no max width beside the 240px sidebar. Grids add columns as the screen widens, board columns stretch, and wide tables scroll inside their card.
